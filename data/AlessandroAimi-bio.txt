Alessandro Aimi is a research fellow at University of Bologna, Italy, where he works on low-power sensor networks, network modeling and simulation, and QoS management automation. He received a PhD in Computer Science from the Conservatoire National des Arts et Métiers (CNAM), France, in 2023, where he was advised by Prof. Stefano Secci, and he received a MSc in Computer Science and Engineering from the Politecnico di Milano in 2020.
