Alessandro Aimi

I'm a research fellow at University of Bologna in Italy, where I am part of the Mobile Middleware Research Group. I did my PhD at CNAM in Paris, France, where I was advised by Prof. Stefano Secci and worked as research scientist at Orange Labs. I received both a MSc and BSc in Computer Science and Engineering from Polytechnic University of Milan, Italy.

Research

I'm interested in low-power IoT networks, network simulation, modeling and optimization, QoS-driven resource allocation, and Cloud infrastructures. I also maintain the lorawan module of the ns-3 network simulator. Some papers are highlighted.

Kubernetes-Based Real-Time LoRaWAN Emulation Toward Network Digital Twins
Alessandro Aimi, Michele Brigadoi, Andrea Sabbioni, Armir Bujari
DS-RT, 2026
bibtex

Integrating ns-3, ELoRa and real network servers in a Kubernetes framework to automate reproducible, real-time emulation and testing of large-scale LoRaWAN deployments.

CLUES: Community-Driven LoRa Networks Urban Sensing Empowered by Smart Homes
Alessandro Aimi, Andrea Sabbioni, Armir Bujari, Paolo Bellavista, Pietro Manzoni
IEEE Network, 2026
bibtex / code

Putting citizen-owned smart LoRa gateways into homes to enable decentralized, community-driven urban sensing with scalable coverage and shared local data services.

ELoRa: End-to-end Emulation of Massive IoT LoRaWAN Infrastructures
Alessandro Aimi, Stéphane Rovedakis, Fabrice Guillemin, Stefano Secci
IEEE/IFIP NOMS, 2023
doi / bibtex / code

ELoRa integrates ns-3 with ChirpStack to emulate realistic LoRaWAN traffic and anomalies at scale, enabling live testing of orchestration and radio resource allocation techniques.

Traffic Control and Channel Assignment for Quality Differentiation in Dense Urban LoRaWANs
Alessandro Aimi, Stéphane Rovedakis, Fabrice Guillemin, Stefano Secci
WiOpt, 2022
doi / bibtex

Traffic control and channel assignment policies can be used for service-quality differentiation in dense LoRaWAN deployments, with improvements in packet delivery, device servicing rates, and network operator revenue.

Packet Delivery Ratio Guarantees for Differentiated LoRaWAN Services
Alessandro Aimi, Stéphane Rovedakis, Fabrice Guillemin, Stefano Secci
IEEE GLOBECOM, 2022
doi / bibtex

An access-control method to isolate device clusters in LoRaWAN and meet differentiated packet delivery targets with improved fairness, at the cost of reduced network range.
